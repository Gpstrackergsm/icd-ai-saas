What We Do — and What We Don't

What We Do

  • Validate ICD-10-CM & HCC logic using deterministic rules
  • Enforce official guidelines (not probabilities)
  • Flag unsupported or inconsistent diagnoses
  • Produce PASS / FAIL decisions with audit-ready reasoning
  • Standardize reviewer decisions at scale

What We Don't Do

  • We do not auto-code
  • We do not replace coders or auditors
  • We do not inflate RAF scores
  • We do not operate as a black box

Designed for Coding & Audit Organizations

This platform is purpose-built for organizations with audit responsibility and RADV exposure.

Target Organizations

  • Coding & Audit Firms
  • Risk Adjustment Vendors
  • RCM Platforms with audit responsibility
  • Organizations exposed to RADV audits

Core Problems We Solve

  • Reviewer-to-reviewer inconsistency
  • Over-conservative vs over-aggressive coding
  • Unsupported HCCs passing internal QA
  • Lack of explainable audit trails during RADV
